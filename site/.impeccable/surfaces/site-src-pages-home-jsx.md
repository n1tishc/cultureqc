- **Job and audience:** a lab-automation VP must understand cultureQC's independent value and fit, then choose whether to sponsor a one-setup offline evaluation. No v2 knowledge is required. Primary action: Explore an analysis. Closing action: Evaluate on your setup.
- **Mode:** Persuade; the visitor understands the product and decides whether to sponsor an offline evaluation. The embedded analysis workspace supports inspection rather than simulated live operation.
- **Implemented composition:** “Read the culture. Understand the next step.” → one actual image/result workspace → Measure/Understand/Trace → intended host boundary → scoped MSC evidence → compact records handoff → offline evaluation. Release history stays in the footer, not the hero or core demo. Navigation: Product (`/#demo`), Evidence (`/validation`), Integration (`/records#integration`), Evaluate on your setup (`/#pilot`). The technical appendix retains legacy targets and opens for their deep links.
- **Implemented interaction:** one Stage with two task entries: Understand a review boundary (default since 2026-10-20: HT29 with evaluation/setup limits); Measure coverage (C2C12 mid-density, no calibrated band). A cited problem statement leads Measure/Understand/Trace; evidence shows the MSC calibration and the experimental fine-tuning count, each with its limit. Raw image/Segmentation switches, reason and exact-record link are visible. Reset restores default selection and contour-only layers; Back/Forward restores selection. All seven examples remain in More examples and by deep link; oversized synthetic contamination is not a default story. Invalid IDs show an explicit fallback notice, and failed assets retain identity with Retry media. No fictional time series, automatic-passage success or recomputed slider outcome.
- **Visual authority and states:** preserve `docs/DESIGN.md` tokens, light reading surface, dark microscopy, cyan segmentation and labelled action colors. Image dominates; result stays adjacent on desktop. Mobile: image, compact result, selector, details. Preserve keyboard focus, reduced motion, deep links and history; handle unavailable assets, invalid selection and failed verification explicitly. No autoplay, decorative KPI claims or fake live progress.
- **Boundaries and acceptance:** distinguish stored/live execution, calibration scope and experimental models. Material limits stay beside outputs; specialist arithmetic and full hashes remain in disclosures. Host owns capture, scheduling, identity/signatures, durable retention and actuation. The current callable is Python, not a deployed host service. Integrity is not scientific validity or compliance; the stored chain contains no review event. No production integration, contamination clearance or ROI claim. Correctness backlog is not solved by visual work. Require desktop/mobile screenshots, native zoom checks and a fresh-reviewer five-minute walkthrough; the script's timings are targets until V3 passes. Preserve the longer console-specific appendix and unrelated user edits.
