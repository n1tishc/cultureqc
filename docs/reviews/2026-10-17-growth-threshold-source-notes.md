# Growth-monitoring thresholds: primary-source notes

Researched 2026-10-17. Scope: evidence-informed pilot choices without a specified deployment microscope, vessel or cell line. These sources describe acquisition or culture practice; none validates cultureQC stall detection or establishes a universal allowable alert delay. No code or active configuration changed.

## Proposed pilot choices, not externally validated thresholds

- **Acquisition proposal:** evaluate a 2 h capture schedule, with 4 h as a lower-burden comparison. These values come from the Sartorius and Bio-protocol acquisition examples above. Use repeated locations and measure actual imaged area; compare 3 versus 5 fields as a source-informed coverage experiment. Their success on Incucyte is not evidence that those field counts suffice for this microscope or vessel.
- **Decision horizon proposal:** if a numeric experimental horizon is needed, compare 12 h and 24 h accumulated growth deficits against a frozen healthy reference. The 12 h arm connects to the repository's existing cadence study; 24 h is an explicitly proposed doubling of that horizon to test whether accumulation improves separation. It is not an ATCC recommendation, an established acceptable delay, or a deployment commitment. Faster image capture and a longer inference window are separate parameters. Record time from confirmed fault onset to first eligible score and first alert, plus missed events; do not hide misses inside a detected-only median.
- **Passage proposal:** select the cell supplier's maintenance protocol before choosing an action band. The source examples justify a configurable target, not a blanket replacement of the existing 80% target. For C2C12, ≤80% is an upper maintenance boundary; prediction, measurement uncertainty and operator response time must be evaluated before approaching it. A measured 80% with uncertainty is not proof that the actual culture is still below that boundary.
- **Lag-phase proposal:** estimate the expected lag period from matched healthy cultures and mark that state explicitly. Do not apply a fixed stall deadline from seeding merely because the acquisition schedule has elapsed. The sources support recognizing lag; they do not supply a transferable numeric suppression period.

## Repository evidence constrains the interpretation

[Growth-signal results](../../results/growth_signal_summary.md) report per-visit growth/noise ratios of 0.35–1.12 across the tested 6 h/12 h and 1/3-FOV settings; larger-field estimates are extrapolations. [SPC results](../../results/spc_summary.md) report 0/2 held-out simulated stalls detected in each setting. The historical criterion was at least 90% detection and median delay no more than 3 visits, with the first post-onset visit counted as 1; that is a repository criterion, not an industry standard.

Consequently, capture more frequent images only as part of a coverage, registration and accumulated-signal experiment. Do not assert that 2 h scans, more fields, a relaxed score limit, or a 24 h horizon fixes the observed failure. Select limits using development cultures, lock the chosen procedure, then measure independent held-out sensitivity, false alerts per culture-time, and delay. Agree operational response tolerance with the pilot owner separately from biological and detector validation.

No source in this bounded review establishes a universal acceptable sensitivity, false-alert budget, minimum FOV count, or growth-stall alert deadline.
