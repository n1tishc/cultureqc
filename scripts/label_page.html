What to paint
  • Cell: every part of a cell, including thin flat edges and long processes, and round bright dividing cells.
  • Background: bare dish, the bright halo around cells, loose debris.
  • Where cells touch, paint straight across; no gaps between them.
  • When unsure about an edge, follow the dark outline of the cell. If a crop is too unclear to judge, skip it.
